# SRD design map

Consult this reference after choosing an SRD route. It maps design-relevant patterns; it is not a substitute for either PDF.

## Official sources

- SRD 5.1, Creative Commons: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.1/SRD_CC_v5.1.pdf
- SRD 5.1, OGL 1.0a: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.1/SRD-OGL_V5.1.pdf
- SRD 5.2.1, CC BY 4.0: https://media.dndbeyond.com/compendium-images/srd/5.2/SRD_CC_v5.2.1.pdf
- Official hub, FAQ, and conversion guide: https://www.dndbeyond.com/srd

SRD 5.1 is available under OGL 1.0a or CC BY 4.0. SRD 5.2.1 is CC BY 4.0 only. Select one route before writing release-facing material.

## What the SRDs provide

They provide a large playable rules foundation: resolution and ability-check patterns, classes and progression, combat and conditions, equipment, spells, monsters, magic items, and encounter guidance. Use a PDF locator for an element that is actually copied or adapted.

## What they do not provide

They do not grant a general right to D&D identity. Do not assume availability of trademarks, logos, artwork, settings, named characters, or all official monsters, classes, species, subclasses, feats, spells, and items. SRD 5.2.1 specifically excludes examples such as the Artificer, Aasimar, and Beholder. It omits names such as Strahd, Orcus, and Tiamat; create original equivalents rather than treating these as reusable.

## Pattern map

| Pattern | Design question |
|---|---|
| d20 plus modifier | Is swingy, bounded success desirable, or would dice pools, cards, or degrees of success better serve the game? |
| advantage/disadvantage | Does roll-twice create clear situational play, or hide probability and stack poorly for this design? |
| action economy | Should turns use discrete actions, AP, simultaneous planning, or fiction-first exchanges? |
| HP and rests | Should attrition, recovery cadence, and injury consequence be central, light, or absent? |
| limited spell slots | Are scarcity and preparation essential, or should powers use cooldowns, stress, risk, or fiction permissions? |
| challenge-rating bands | Is party-versus-threat estimation sufficient, or does the game need scenario, objective, morale, or terrain-first encounter design? |

Use these as options to reuse, modify, combine, or reject. Copying the full class/level, six-ability-score, spell-slot, long-rest, and CR stack unchanged will make the result strongly D&D-shaped; justify that choice or redesign the pattern.

## 5.1 to 5.2.1 changes relevant to design

SRD 5.2.1 updates wording and mechanics to the 5.5e rules. The official conversion material identifies added or revised species and backgrounds, weapon masteries, an expanded rules glossary, updated monster stat blocks, new and renamed content, and additional spells, feats, items, and travel/environmental material. Use the official conversion guide or PDF locators for exact deltas; do not infer compatibility.

## Included / excluded / uncertain check

Before release:
- Is the element present in the selected SRD version, and is its locator recorded?
- Does the chosen license route cover the way it is used?
- Does it include a trademark, setting name, named character, logo, artwork, unique monster, or non-SRD expression?
- Is it merely a familiar mechanical pattern that can be expressed originally instead?
- If uncertain, redesign and rename it or obtain qualified review.

This is an operational content boundary, not legal advice.
