- personal use, hobby projects, self-directed learning
- student and academic use
- use by educational institutions, charities, public research bodies and

- forking, modifying and redistributing for any of the above, provided the
  licence and the Required Notice travel with the copy

## What needs a commercial licence

Anything commercial. In practice that means:

- use inside a company developing, publishing or operating a game
- use in consulting, contracting or agency work delivered to clients
- use in paid training, paid workshops or paid courses
- redistribution or resale of these skills, in whole or in part, including
  repackaging them for any agent-skill marketplace
